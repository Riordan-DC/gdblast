Guide to getting this bullshit to build

First: this shit isnt maintained well.

1. Packman. If you download nvblast it comes with an old version of nvidias package manager packman. Their
gitlab link doesnt work so I cant download a newer packman. Luckily PhysX is better maintained and includes a newer
packman. I copied that in and modified the build scripts so it could compile. This whole endevour was a waste of time
because in the end I just ended up copying the sdk and only compiling libs and not samples. Its too many depedencies.

2. This was built to work with VS 2017 and v141 toolset. Nobody uses that so I use VS 2022 and whatever new toolset. 
This causes a minor but easy to fix error in PhysX shared where you must change typeinfo.h to just typeinfo. No big deal.

3. I needed the extension for shaders, asset utils, and stress. This required a small modification to change
std::pow to just pow.
